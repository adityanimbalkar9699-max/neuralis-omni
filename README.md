## 🚀 Quick Start Guide (Run in 10 Seconds)

### Step 1: Start the Local Continuity Server
From the project root:
```powershell
python serve.py
```
This automatically detects your local Wi-Fi IP and outputs:
- **Local Laptop URL**: `http://localhost:8080`
- **Mobile Phone URL**: `http://<YOUR-LAN-IP>:8080`

### Step 2: Open on Your Laptop
Open your browser to:
[http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)

### Step 3: Open on Your Smartphone
1. Connect your smartphone to the same Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot.
2. Click **[PHONE SYNC]** in the app to display the QR code, or type the mobile URL in your phone browser (Safari, Chrome, Brave).
3. The app will open full-screen with native touch gestures and camera support!
